Poultry management

Keep housing clean, provide safe drinking water and suitable feed, maintain ventilation and observe birds daily. Follow a vaccination and health plan with a qualified poultry professional.

🐐

Livestock nutrition

Choose feed for the animal species, age and production purpose. Introduce diet changes carefully and ask a qualified animal nutritionist or veterinarian about specific formulations.

🌾

Feed storage and quality

Store feed in a cool, dry, clean place, raised off damp floors and protected from pests and rain. Do not feed mouldy, contaminated or unusual-smelling products.

🧼

Farm hygiene and biosecurity

Clean equipment, limit unnecessary visitors to animal areas, practise footwear and hand hygiene, and seek professional help promptly when animals become sick.

🌱

Crop production basics

Choose suitable seeds for local soil, season and conditions. Plan land preparation, weeds, water and pests carefully, and use crop-protection products only as their labels and safety rules direct.

📒

Farm records and planning

Track purchases, feed use, animal health, production, sales and expenses. Simple records help you spot changes and make better farm decisions.

Customer Hub · quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for shopping and using Iyanda One. If an order-specific detail is not shown here, ask the team before making payment.

How do I place an order?

Browse Shop & Products, add items to your cart and submit the checkout form. Review the order details and wait for Iyanda to confirm availability, delivery and the final amount.

Are all products always in stock?

No. Stock and availability can change. The team confirms current availability before an order is fulfilled.

Can I pay online inside Iyanda One?

Eligible NGN orders can be paid securely through Paystack after the order amount and delivery fee are confirmed. Demo/test products, unpriced products and orders awaiting a delivery quotation cannot be paid online. Payment is confirmed only after Paystack verification. Never pay into an unverified account.

How are delivery charges calculated?

Delivery coverage and charges depend on your destination and order. The team confirms the delivery fee and final total before payment.

Can I collect my order?

Collection may be available by arrangement. Select collection at checkout and wait for confirmation of the collection details before travelling.

How do I track my order?

Sign in and open My Account to view orders linked to your account. For a fulfilment update, contact Iyanda with your order number. Live courier tracking is not currently provided.

What if an item is missing or damaged?

Contact support with your order number, the affected item, a short description and clear photos where useful. The team will review and advise on the next step.

How does customer loyalty work?

Earn 0.5% of the product subtotal on eligible completed NGN orders. Referral rewards are awarded after a qualifying completed order. Sign in to view your balance and referral activity.

Can I place wholesale or bulk orders?

Yes. Send the products, quantity, pack size, location and preferred date to the Iyanda team for a quotation.

How do I contact support?

Use Contact & Support in this app, or contact the team on WhatsApp at +234 704 307 1733 or by email at iyandafarmsltd@gmail.com.

How do I manage my account?

Use My Account in the app to sign in, update your profile, review your orders and support requests, or sign out.
