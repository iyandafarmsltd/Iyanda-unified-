Poultry management

Keep housing clean, provide safe drinking water and suitable feed, maintain ventilation and observe birds daily. Follow a vaccination and health plan with a qualified poultry professional.

🐐

Livestock nutrition

Choose feed for the animal species, age and production purpose. Introduce diet changes carefully and ask a qualified animal nutritionist or veterinarian about specific formulations.

🌾

Feed storage and quality

Store feed in a cool, dry, clean place, raised off damp floors and protected from pests and rain. Do not feed mouldy, contaminated or unusual-smelling products.

🧼

Farm hygiene and biosecurity

Clean equipment, limit unnecessary visitors to animal areas, practise footwear and hand hygiene, and seek professional help promptly when animals become sick.

🌱

Crop production basics

Choose suitable seeds for local soil, season and conditions. Plan land preparation, weeds, water and pests carefully, and use crop-protection products only as their labels and safety rules direct.

📒

Farm records and planning

Track purchases, feed use, animal health, production, sales and expenses. Simple records help you spot changes and make better farm decisions.
