1. Privacy Notice

Who operates the platform: Iyanda One is operated by Iyanda Farm Foods & Feed Ltd, based in Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. Contact iyandafarmsltd@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 704 307 1733 for privacy questions or requests.

Information we handle

  • Account and profile information, such as your name, email address, phone number, country and region.
  • Order, payment-reference, delivery-address and fulfilment information needed to process purchases and provide support.
  • Seller and delivery-partner application details, business information and operational records.
  • Messages, support requests, reviews and information you choose to submit to platform tools.
  • Content and prompts you submit when using AI, language, creative or productivity features.
  • Basic platform usage and page-view analytics used to understand service performance.

How information is used and shared

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide platform features, process orders and payments, coordinate delivery or collection, support customers and partners, prevent abuse, maintain records and improve the service.

Payments are handled through Paystack. Iyanda One stores payment references and transaction status needed to reconcile orders; payment-card details are handled by the payment provider rather than entered into Iyanda's own order database. Supabase provides account and database services. PostHog is configured for basic page-view analytics; automatic interaction capture and session recording are disabled in the current configuration.

When needed to fulfil an order, relevant order and contact details may be shared with the seller or delivery partner responsible for that order. Service providers may process information to provide hosting, database, payment, analytics or AI services. We do not publish private customer records or sell personal information as a business practice.

International processing

Iyanda One serves users in different countries. Information may be processed outside your country by service providers. The current Supabase project is hosted in the EU West region and the PostHog analytics endpoint is in the United States. Provider locations and infrastructure may change as services evolve.

Storage, security and retention

We use account access controls, database permissions and security measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may ask to access or correct your information, or request account deletion, by contacting us. Some order, payment, fraud-prevention or legal records may need to be retained where required or reasonably necessary. We will review requests and explain any limits that apply.

Your choices

Provide only information needed for the feature you use. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or highly sensitive personal information into AI prompts or support messages. You can contact us to ask about the information associated with your account or to raise a privacy concern.

2. Platform Terms

By accessing or using Iyanda One, you agree to use the platform lawfully and responsibly. You must provide accurate account, order and application information, keep your sign-in details secure, and not impersonate another person, interfere with the platform, misuse another user's information or attempt to bypass security controls.

Marketplace and orders

Product and service listings are subject to availability, seller confirmation and applicable fulfilment conditions. Sellers are responsible for the accuracy and legality of their listings and for fulfilling accepted orders. Customers should review the item, quantity, price, currency, delivery or collection details and order total before paying.

For delivery orders, the delivery charge may be confirmed separately by Iyanda through WhatsApp after the order is placed. A delivery charge not shown in the checkout total is not included in the amount paid at checkout; any separately confirmed charge must be agreed before dispatch. Collection orders do not incur a delivery charge unless a separate written arrangement says otherwise.

Payments and availability

Online payments are processed by the payment provider and an order is marked paid only after payment verification. The payment methods and currencies available depend on the transaction and provider support. International trade quotations, shipping arrangements and payments may have additional transaction-specific conditions.

We work to keep the platform available and accurate but do not guarantee uninterrupted access, error-free operation, uninterrupted AI output or availability of every feature in every country. Features may be updated, limited or temporarily unavailable for maintenance, security or operational reasons.

AI and user content

AI-generated answers, translations, recommendations and creative outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate. They are provided for general assistance and are not professional legal, medical, financial or safety advice. Check important decisions with an appropriately qualified professional. You remain responsible for the content you submit and for checking outputs before relying on or publishing them.

Account restrictions

We may restrict access where reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate suspected fraud, respond to unlawful activity, enforce these terms or maintain platform security. Where appropriate, we will provide an explanation and a way to contact support.

3. Refunds & Cancellations

If an order is incorrect, incomplete, damaged, missing or not received, or you need to request cancellation or a refund, submit an after-sales request from the Customer Hub or contact Iyanda using the support details below. Include your order reference and a clear description. Photos or other supporting information may help us review the case.

  • Cancellation requests should be made as early as possible. A request made before dispatch can be reviewed; an order already dispatched may not be cancellable.
  • Missing, damaged, incorrect or undelivered items will be reviewed against the order and available fulfilment information.
  • Refunds are subject to review and approval. Where approved, the refund will normally be returned through the original payment route where the provider supports it.
  • Any refund timing shown by the payment provider or bank is outside Iyanda's direct control.
  • Delivery charges already incurred or third-party costs may be considered during review and will be explained in the decision.

Submitting a request does not automatically mean a refund has been approved. We will communicate the outcome or request further information where needed. Statutory consumer rights that apply to you are not excluded by this policy.

4. Delivery & Collection

Delivery availability depends on the destination, product, seller and available delivery network. A delivery estimate is not a guarantee unless expressly confirmed in writing. Customers must provide a complete and reachable delivery address and a working contact number.

Where the delivery charge is not included in checkout, Iyanda will communicate the applicable quote separately, normally through WhatsApp. The fee and serviceability should be agreed before dispatch. Customers may choose collection where that option is offered for the order.

Delivery partners may receive the contact and destination details needed to fulfil an assigned order. Customers may be asked to provide a delivery confirmation code or acknowledge receipt. Keep delivery codes private until the order is being handed over to you or your authorised recipient.

If delivery cannot be completed because the address is inaccurate, the recipient is unavailable or the destination is inaccessible, the team may contact you to arrange the next step. Any additional attempt or return charge will be communicated before it is applied.

5. Seller Agreement

Marketplace sellers must provide truthful business and contact details, accurate product descriptions, genuine prices and stock information, and comply with laws and safety requirements that apply to their goods and services.

  • Do not list prohibited, counterfeit, unsafe, misleading or unlawful products.
  • Keep stock and prices current, respond to order enquiries and fulfil accepted orders in a timely and professional manner.
  • Protect customer information and use it only to fulfil the relevant order or meet a legal obligation.
  • Do not manipulate reviews, orders, referrals, rewards or platform records, or move platform-generated transactions off-platform to avoid agreed terms.
  • Cooperate with reasonable order, complaint, refund and fraud reviews.

The standard marketplace commission currently recorded in the seller onboarding flow is 10% of eligible sales where the seller has accepted those commission terms. The applicable accepted terms and transaction records govern the calculation. Seller earnings and payouts remain subject to completed-order status, payment reconciliation, refunds, disputes and the platform's payout review controls.

Seller approval is required before marketplace selling features are activated. Iyanda may pause a listing or seller account where necessary to investigate a complaint, protect customers or enforce these requirements.

6. Delivery Partner Agreement

Delivery partners must provide accurate identity, business, contact, coverage and payout information and must complete the platform's approval process before accepting assigned deliveries.

  • Accept and fulfil only deliveries assigned to your approved account and within the agreed coverage and service terms.
  • Keep customer names, phone numbers, addresses, order details and delivery codes confidential. Use them only to complete the assigned delivery.
  • Provide accurate status updates and delivery evidence when requested. Never claim an order was delivered when it was not.
  • Follow safe handling practices, applicable transport laws and reasonable customer-care standards.
  • Report failed deliveries, loss, damage, safety concerns or suspected fraud promptly.

Partner rates, payment basis and settlement arrangements are agreed individually and recorded through the platform or a separate written agreement. No universal partner rate or payout date is promised by this page. Settlements are subject to completed delivery records, review and the agreed terms.
