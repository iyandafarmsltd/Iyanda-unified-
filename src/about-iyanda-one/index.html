Meet Iyanda One

Iyanda One is the connected digital platform developed by Iyanda Farm Foods & Feed Ltd, an Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria-based business. It brings marketplace commerce, agriculture, global trade, AI assistance, learning and everyday digital tools together in one place.

What you can explore

  • Marketplace and commerce: discover products and services, explore shopping features and connect with sellers.
  • Agriculture and farm activities: access farm-related information and agricultural features connected with the Iyanda ecosystem.
  • AI and digital tools: explore AI assistance, productivity, creativity, language and learning tools.
  • Global opportunities: use a platform designed to serve people and businesses across regions, subject to feature availability and local requirements.

About the operator

Iyanda Farm Foods & Feed Ltd operates from Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. The business is associated with fresh produce, food, livestock, poultry and animal-feed activities. Iyanda One extends the brand into a broader digital platform.
